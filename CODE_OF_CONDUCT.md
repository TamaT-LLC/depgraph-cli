# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a harassment-free, inclusive, and professional project community.
Participation is welcome regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of expected behavior include constructive technical disagreement,
respect for boundaries, clear attribution, patient help, and accepting
responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption,
impersonation, spam, or publishing another person's private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release and support channels, project events, and when someone officially
represents depgraph or TamaT-LLC.

For an ordinary moderation request that is safe to discuss publicly, flag the
specific conversation for a maintainer. For a confidential report, use the
private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), identify it as a code-of-conduct
report, and include only the information needed to investigate. Do not publish
sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Organization-assigned conduct maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a
private warning, limit participation, temporarily suspend participation, ban a
participant, close or lock a conversation, or escalate to the TamaT-LLC
organization owner. They must protect reporter privacy, disclose conflicts,
recuse when conflicted, and document the reason for material enforcement
actions in a restricted record.

An affected person may appeal once to the TamaT-LLC organization owner through
the same private route. The appeal reviewer must not be the sole person who
made the original decision. Retaliation against a reporter, witness, or
reviewer is prohibited.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
